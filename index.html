Services

What we do

Six things, each one written out properly instead of a heading on its own.

A/C repair

Not cooling, tripping the breaker, freezing up or blowing warm. We diagnose it, tell you what it is and what it costs before we touch it.

Heater repair

Furnaces and heat strips that will not fire, short-cycle or smell wrong. Worth testing before the first cold snap rather than during it.

System installation

Full change-outs, sized properly for the house rather than sold by the ton. We explain what each option costs to run, not just to buy.

Heat pump repair

Heat pumps that will not switch over, ice up in winter or run constantly. Common in this part of Louisiana and worth getting right.

Fan motor replacement

Blower and condenser fan motors, capacitors and contactors — the parts that fail most often and are usually the cheapest fix on the list.

Duct cleaning & repair

Leaking, crushed or disconnected duct work makes a good system behave like a bad one. Often the reason one room never cools.
